How to play

One player holds the phone sideways against their forehead, with the screen facing the team. The team gives clues and the holder says the answer.

Flip up (screen to the ceiling) for Got it. Flip down (screen to the floor) to Pass. When time runs out, the team reviews the turn and the phone passes to the next team.

Common questions

Do I need the internet or an account?

No. The whole game runs on your phone.

What if flipping is hard for someone?

Switch scoring to Buttons, and a teammate taps Got it or Pass instead. You can also change the flip sensitivity in Settings.

Why is there no sound?

By default, the game follows your phone's Silent switch. To hear sounds anyway, turn on Play sounds even when muted in Settings. Also check that Sound is on.

How do I end a turn early?

Press and hold End on the turn screen until the circle fills.

What happens if a call comes in?

The turn pauses, and you can resume it afterward.

How do I erase my data?

Delete the app. Everything it saved is on your phone and goes with it. See the privacy policy.

Flip Wits

A family party game for the phone on your forehead.

Hold the phone to your forehead with the screen facing your team. They shout clues, you say the answer, and you flip the phone to score. Play with numbers, words and more, with students and grown-ups on the same team.

Flip Wits is in testing and not yet on the App Store.

How to play

One phone, one holder, and a team shouting clues.

  • 42

    Hold it up

    One player holds the phone sideways against their forehead, with the screen facing the team.

  • Got it

    Flip up for Got it

    The team gives clues and the holder says the answer. Tip the screen to the ceiling to score.

  • Pass

    Flip down to Pass

    Tip the screen to the floor to skip. When time runs out, the team reviews the turn and the phone moves on.

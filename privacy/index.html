Flip Wits Support Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Flip Wits collects nothing about you. We have no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the app does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your phone

The app saves your settings, team names, scores and any game in progress on your phone. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

Motion sensors

The app reads the phone's motion sensors during a turn to notice when you flip it. Those readings are used in the moment. They are not saved or sent anywhere.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party code that collects data, and we do not sell or share data, because we have none.

Information from Apple

Apple handles App Store downloads and TestFlight under its own privacy policy. If you turn on sharing with app developers in your iPhone's settings, or send feedback through TestFlight, Apple may pass along crash reports, feedback or summary usage figures. We use those only to fix and improve the app.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Deleting your data

Delete the app. That removes everything it saved on your phone.

If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and what you send, and we use them only to reply.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
